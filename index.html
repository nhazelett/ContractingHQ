Announcements

Recent updates, new features, and changes to ContractingHQ.

May 4, 2026New
Customer Education suite is complete. All sixteen pages of the Customer Education track are live. Topics span package basics, describing the need, schedules, acceptance criteria, deliverables (with a CDRL deeper dive), evaluation factors, IGCEs, single-source evidence, recompetes, COR nominations, contract changes, and performance failures. Built for program managers, requiring activities, and resource folks who hand work to COs — start at the hub.
May 4, 2026New
Acquisition Package Preflight. A new browser-only package readiness checker is live. Answer structured questions, keep sensitive details out of the tool, and get a printable memo with showstoppers, gaps, strengths, and KTHQ links to fix the package before solicitation.
Apr 29, 2026New
SAT Warrant Study Guide. A study guide tailored for SAT-level warrants is live. Same study format as the unlimited-warrant guide but pitched at the actual scope and decision-making a SAT-level CO will face on a board. Lower knowledge ceiling, less Part 15 deep cuts, more bread-and-butter Part 12 / Part 13 / commercial item rhythm.
Apr 29, 2026New
CCO Capstone builders. UDMs and UTMs, new training swag is up. Four document generators are live: Form 9, 1449 / SF30, 1442, and BPA. Use them to build injects, sample contracts, or prior workload to populate a fictional shop for hands-on training. Heads up: clauses are illustrative and not vetted, so don't reuse any of this in a real package. Find them on the Tools page under UDM Tools.
Apr 26, 2026New
Industry track is live. Four new pages aimed at small businesses and new federal contractors: how to register in SAM, the SAM renewal scam (don't get scammed), reps and certs from a CO's perspective, and how to invoice through WAWF. More on the way. Start at the Industry hub.
Apr 26, 2026New
CORs landing page. Created a place for Contracting Officer's Representatives. Indexed the existing COR training (annual file reviews, Type A, contract-specific) and added a roadmap of what's coming. CORs vastly outnumber COs and deserve their own track.
