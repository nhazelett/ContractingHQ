ASK THE MARKET

Industry engagement

Record public research takeaways from RFIs, industry days, demonstrations, and vendor discussions.

Questions worth asking industry
  • Which existing products or services meet the outcome? What would require modification?
  • What limits competition unnecessarily in the draft requirement?
  • What are normal lead times, commercial terms, minimum order quantities, and cost drivers?
  • What integration, licensing, data-rights, cybersecurity, and sustainment issues should we plan for?
  • What capability evidence, demonstrations, and references can be verified?
  • What can small businesses perform directly, and where would teaming be needed?
