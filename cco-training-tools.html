CCO Capstone

CCO Training Tools

Builders for generating realistic supporting contract documents. Use them to populate a fake "contracting office file cabinet" so trainees walk into a Capstone with prior workload to dig through.

← Back to UDM

How to use these

Pick a scenario, then build the paper trail backward. A new BPA implies older Form 9s that justified its standup. A 1449 award implies a market research package, a justification, and a funding cite. Generate each artifact, save the PDFs, and stack them into a folder by fictional fiscal year.

When trainees show up for the Capstone, hand them the folder. That's the office. Injects you drop later land harder when there is real prior workload to react to — the BPA holder going out of business, an option year coming due, a CDR finding from last quarter.

All builders generate downloadable PDFs locally in your browser. Nothing is saved on the site — collect them in a folder of your own and zip the office up when you're done.

T-01 Form 9 Builder Request for Purchase. The customer-side document that kicks off a buy — requirement, funding, justification, signatures. Build a stack of these to represent prior years' demand. Live T-02 1449 / SF30 Builder Full SF 1449 face page plus UCF Sections A through M, CLIN editor with auto-totals, clause selector, and a separate SF 30 modification builder. Save drafts locally, export JSON, print to PDF. Live T-03 1442 Builder Full SF 1442 solicitation/offer/award package with UCF Sections A through M, construction CLIN editor, FAR 52.236-series clause selector, and a separate SF 30 mod builder. Live T-04 BPA Builder Master BPA package plus BPA call generator (OF 347-style). Open-market or FSS BPAs, call-level funding, RFO BPA language, and WAWF references. Live T-05 IDIQ Builder Indefinite-Delivery / Indefinite-Quantity contract framework with ceiling, guaranteed minimum, order limitations, 52.216-series clauses, and task/delivery order placeholders.
