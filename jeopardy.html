Pick a clue. Make your call.

Choose a category and dollar value, read the clue, then reveal the answer. A correct answer adds points; an incorrect answer subtracts them. Skip a regular clue for no points. The countdown is a guide: you can still reveal and score after it ends.

Finish the first board to unlock Double Jeopardy, with double values and two Daily Doubles. A Daily Double belongs to the player who selected it: wager first, then score the answer. In multiplayer, choose the player who answered correctly or incorrectly; everyone self-scores on the honor system.

Final Jeopardy has one category and one clue. Players with a positive score lock in a wager, then reveal the answer and score each player. Each new game draws different clues from the reviewed bank.

300 reviewed clues · Three rounds · Solo or up to four players
