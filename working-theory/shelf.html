How to get these free

Open the DoD collection in your browser, or add “Department of Defense” in Libby, choose “Sign In With My Card,” select “DoD MWR Libraries,” and authenticate with your DoD ID and birthdate. No physical library card is needed. Active duty, Guard, Reserve, retirees, and eligible sponsored family members can use the collection. DoD civilian access depends on location and resource availability. Official setup instructions and eligibility details.

Each book will link straight to its title in the DoD collection. If unavailable there, its source entry will say so and link to the publisher.
