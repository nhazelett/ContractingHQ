How this data works

Where available, this office loads a completed nightly USAspending snapshot. The Updated stamp shows its publication time in Eastern time; it is not a live feed. Advanced → Data source lets you use the original live loader. Loading instructions below describe that live path.

Coverage and freshness

USAspending public data may be delayed, particularly DoD reporting. Search complete means all returned search pages for this office prefix and action window have been checked; it does not certify a complete office inventory. Missing award detail records remain visible for review.

Office assignment uses the first six characters of the PIID. Source actions in the selected history window determine candidate coverage; older awards without activity in that window may be absent. Office names come from the supplied FY22–26 directory.

Datasets and filters

Active / upcoming includes unresolved dates. Potentially completed means the reported potential final end has passed, not confirmed performance completion, acceptance, closeout, or recompete. Needs date review isolates missing, conflicting, pending, or unavailable date evidence. The All view combines every loaded record.

Fiscal totals and the Active / upcoming headline are office-wide. Other portfolio views follow the selected dataset; end-date views also follow the selected date basis. Recompete candidates always use potential final ends. Register search and status filters are local to that register and its CSV export.

Dates and options

Current PoP and potential final end remain separate source dates. A potential end may include unexercised options; it is not an established option schedule or confirmed exercise. No option count or current option year is inferred. Records with uncertain dates may appear in the register but are excluded from upcoming-end lists when the date evidence is insufficient.

The public transaction endpoint does not provide per-mod PoP dates. Reported option-exercise reason codes establish an action, not the resulting option period. Same-day records, missing history, and gaps limit sequencing and attribution. “None Found” describes the available history, not proof that no modifications or options exist.

Amounts, fiscal years, and set-asides

Fiscal figures are net obligations, including deobligations, across the full prior FY and current FY to date. Contract counts are distinct awards with reported activity, not only newly awarded contracts. Totals stay blank until the fiscal scan completes. Fiscal years begin October 1.

Current value is the source’s cumulative base-and-exercised-options value, not potential value or obligations. No set-aside used does not establish full and open competition. Not reported is distinct from Other. Set-aside labels reflect the latest source record, not a vendor certification or reconstruction of every previous action.

Loading and export

Only the selected office is loaded, automatically and in paced requests. Pause and Resume retain loaded records in this page. The session cache lasts up to six hours; refreshing starts a new page session. Retry delays respect source responses. CSV includes all filtered register matches, not just its displayed page.

While your office loads

A full portfolio can take several minutes to load, especially for larger offices. You can explore results as they arrive; counts and totals are partial until loading finishes. Keep this page open, or use Pause and Resume.
