Air Show in a Box

Plan it. Buy it. Defend the file.

Everything a contracting officer and an open house project officer need to take an Air Force air show from a date on the calendar to signed awards. Each step shows the rule it comes from, so you can pull the reference and read it yourself.

New to this? Start here.

An air show is not one contract. It is twenty or more small buys that all have to land on the same weekend: acts, an Air Boss, sound, fuel, toilets, buses, fencing. Three things trip people up:

  • Money. Some of it can only be bought with appropriated funds and some of it cannot be bought with appropriated funds at all.
  • Authority. Only a contracting officer can commit the Government to a performer or vendor. A handshake by the project officer is an unauthorized commitment.
  • Time. A few dates are set by regulation and cannot be compressed, such as the FAA waiver.

Work the five steps in order the first time. After that, jump to whatever you need.

Which money pays for what

This decides the rulebook before anything else. Appropriated fund buys follow the FAR. NAF and sponsorship buys follow DAFMAN 64-119, and the FAR does not apply to them. When in doubt, ask FM and FSS: the manual tells you to.

Is it a warbird?

For any flying act this is the first question, because it decides the money. A warbird can be paid with appropriated funds. Any other civilian act cannot. The manual lists approved warbird types in Attachment 6, and the installation commander approves warbird participation.

From requirement to award

The path for an appropriated fund buy under the simplified acquisition threshold, which is where almost every air show buy sits. Of air show awards on record, were at or under $350,000.

    NAF buys take a different path. A warranted NAF contracting officer or the servicing contracting office makes the award under DAFMAN 64-119: no competition required at $10,000 or less, two sources over $10,000, three over $250,000, and a written justification from the requesting activity for a sole source.
