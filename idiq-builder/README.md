# IDIQ Generator

A local, static training tool for building simulated indefinite-delivery indefinite-quantity contract packages.

Open `index.html` in a browser. The app includes:

- SF 1449-style face page for commercial product or commercial service IDIQ training samples.
- UCF continuation pages for Sections B through J, plus solicitation-only Sections L/M.
- Editable IDIQ ceiling, guaranteed minimum, minimum order, maximum order limits, ordering period, and final completion date.
- Dynamic 52.216-series clause set:
  - `52.216-18` Ordering
  - `52.216-19` Order Limitations
  - `52.216-22` Indefinite Quantity
  - `52.216-27` Single or Multiple Awards when a multiple-award solicitation is selected
  - `52.216-32` Task-Order and Delivery-Order Ombudsman when multiple-award IDIQ is selected
- Deployed Air Force defaults for FA4867 PIIDs, Form 9 number, contacts, addresses, DFAS Columbus, and `20XX` training dates.
- Contractor roster with simulated UEI and CAGE values.
- Signature toggles, JSON import/export, local draft save, and browser Save to PDF.

This is a training simulator. It does not create an official government contract, order, procurement record, or system-of-record entry. Current FAR/DFARS text and agency procedures should be verified before real-world use.
