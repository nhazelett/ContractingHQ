Contingency Contracting

Training and references for COs operating in deployed, austere, or rapid-response environments. The mission is the same: buy what the Government needs. The rules flex, the timelines compress, and the file works differently.

Contingency contracting is acquisition done in conditions that don't look like garrison work. Deployed locations, declared contingencies, humanitarian response, fire and flood support — anywhere routine procurement procedures have to bend to operational reality. Contingency Contracting Officers (CCOs) operate inside an ecosystem of authorities, forms, and roles that don't show up in day-to-day garrison contracting: FAR Part 18 flexibilities, manual contract writing, SF44 purchases, Field Ordering Officers acting downrange, and the Operational Contract Support framework that ties it all together.
