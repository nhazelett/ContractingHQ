Why GAO decisions matter

The FAR tells you what you have to do. It rarely tells you how. GAO bid protest decisions show how the rules actually get used in disputes: which arguments win, which fail, and which patterns to avoid in your own files.

Use the feed below as a quick browse. The real value comes from going to GAO directly. The bid protest search page lets you filter by clause or topic. Curious what's been protested under FAR 52.217-9? Search it. As you grow as a CO, this is how you step out of the regulation and see how it plays out in the wild.
