Why work from a list

  • The acts get the attention. The support buys sink the schedule. Toilets, buses, fencing and sound each need their own requirement, funds and award, and they all come due at once.
  • Each requirement belongs to somebody. The contracting officer buys it. The owning unit defines it and funds it. If nobody owns it, it does not get bought.
  • Identify purchase requirements early and put a contracting officer on the open house staff. That is the manual's instruction, not a courtesy.

Card or contract: the $2,500 question

A buy at or under the micro-purchase threshold needs no competitive quotes, and the Government purchase card is a primary way to pay for it. The threshold is $15,000. For a service covered by the service contract labor standards it is $2,500. Most air show buys sit between those two numbers, so which one applies decides how most of the show gets bought.

What decides it

  • Supplies are not services. Smoke oil and fuel delivered to the base are supplies.
  • The standards cover services performed through service employees. Where the contractor performs the service individually, and the contracting officer knows that when soliciting or concluding negotiations, the standards are treated as not applying.
  • Calling the crew independent contractors changes nothing. Anyone performing the work is a service employee regardless of the contractual relationship claimed.
  • Being a pilot is not an exemption. Labor's rules name pilots on an air ambulance contract as service employees, not exempt professionals. The solo performer rule above is what applies to a one-person act, not the pilot's profession.
  • The $2,500 figure is set by statute. The inflation adjustments that raised the general threshold to $15,000 do not reach it.

What bases on record did

Solicitations on record with a wage determination attached: parking 11 of 15, Air Boss 12 of 27, announcer 8 of 22, aerial acts 16 of 55, pyrotechnics 8 of 30, smoke oil 0 of 24. Offices split on the solo performers and were consistent on the crews and the supplies.

Limits that stay in place either way

  • Local purchase card procedures can set a lower single-purchase limit for services. The agency program coordinator has that answer.
  • These thresholds are for appropriated funds. NAF buys follow their own dollar levels.
  • A civil performer still provides the hold harmless agreement and certificate of insurance for a landing permit, however the act is paid.
  • Only a contracting officer or a cardholder acting within their authority commits the Government. The director does not book the act.
