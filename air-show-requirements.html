Requirements Library

Every kind of buy that shows up at a military air show, pulled from thousands of real awards. Open one to see who usually writes the requirement, what money pays for it, what bases paid, and what other bases put in their solicitations. Tick the ones your show needs and the page builds your buy plan.

Why work from a list

  • The acts get the attention. The support buys sink the schedule. Toilets, buses, fencing and sound each need their own requirement, funds and award, and they all come due at once.
  • Each requirement belongs to somebody. The contracting officer buys it. The owning unit defines it and funds it. If nobody owns it, it does not get bought.
  • Identify purchase requirements early and put a contracting officer on the open house staff. That is the manual's instruction, not a courtesy.
